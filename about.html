📖 About Vidya Vihar School

Suryadeo Pandey

Principal, Vidya Vihar School

"Education is not merely a tool for earning a living, but the foundation of an enlightened society. At Vidya Vihar School, our core belief is that distance or financial constraints should never become a barrier for any child's dreams. We are committed to reaching remote areas through our dedicated transport and keeping quality education affordable, ensuring that no deserving student is ever left behind."

Located in the serene surroundings of Basantpur Road, Katkamsandi, Vidya Vihar School is dedicated to molding young minds with strong moral values, discipline, and academic excellence. Affiliated under JAC norms (JAC Code: 210960 | UDISE Code: 20041003611), our institution acts as a second home where every student is nurtured to realize their true potential.

🌱 Our Eco-Friendly Campus

Our campus is richly adorned with greenery, trees, and plants, providing a peaceful and pollution-free atmosphere. This natural environment fosters healthy thinking and instills a deep sense of environmental responsibility in our students.

🚌 Bridging Distances with Accessible Transport

Understanding the challenges faced by families in remote pockets, we operate dedicated school bus and van facilities. This ensures safe, timely, and comfortable commuting for students coming from distant areas, making quality education accessible right at their doorstep.

🌟 Why Parents Choose Us

  • ✅ Affordable & Quality Education: Premium learning standards maintained at an economical fee structure.
  • ✅ Reaching Remote Learners: Strong transport connectivity bringing students from far-flung rural areas to regular schooling.
  • ✅ Experienced Faculty: Passionate teachers dedicated to individual student attention and moral character building.
  • ✅ Holistic Growth & School Functions: Equal emphasis on academics, discipline, and regular cultural events / functions on special occasions where students actively participate to build confidence.

Together with our parents and community, we stride forward with a vision to empower every child for a brighter and more secure tomorrow.
